The idea

Built to be understood, not memorised

Three promises behind every explainer on this platform.

Who is this for?

Built for engineers who want to go deep — not skim the surface.

If you can read code but the math and intuition behind AI/ML still feel like a black box, you are exactly who this is for. No prior AI background assumed — just curiosity and the willingness to dig in.

What you will do?

You will interact with the content — click buttons, drag sliders, step through every animation — until each concept clicks and stays.

No passive walls of text. Every idea is something you poke at, break, and rebuild, so the understanding is yours to keep.

What you will achieve?

In-depth understanding of every topic, from the ground up to genuine mastery.

From first principles to the frontier — one carefully built idea at a time, with nothing hand-waved along the way.

Optional · AdvancedMany explainers also include optional advanced content — from companion Jupyter notebooks to full end-to-end projects — for readers who want to roll up their sleeves and build everything from scratch.
